Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Who We Are

Stories by Abhinav is a wedding and portrait photography studio based in Sundernagar, Himachal Pradesh, India. This policy explains what personal information we collect through sbabhinav.com, why we collect it and what we do with it. If you have any questions, email us at storiesbyabhinavv@gmail.com.

What We Collect

We only collect what you choose to send us. When you fill in the contact form, we receive:

  • your name, phone number and email address
  • the type of shoot you're interested in
  • your event date and location, if you share them
  • anything you write in your message

What We Don't Collect

We don't add analytics, advertising trackers or tracking cookies to this website, and we don't build profiles of visitors. The embedded YouTube films are the one exception to this, as explained below. You can browse every page without telling us anything about yourself.

How We Use Your Information

We use the details you send to reply to your enquiry, check our availability, prepare a quote and, if you book with us, plan and deliver your shoot. We don't sell, rent or share your information with anyone for marketing, and we won't add you to a mailing list.

Services That Handle Your Data

We rely on a few trusted services to run this website. Each one handles only the data it needs:

  • Formspree receives contact form submissions and forwards them to our email inbox. Formspree is based in the United States, so your submission may be processed there.
  • Gmail (Google) is where enquiries arrive and where we reply to you.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like most web hosts, it may log basic technical details such as your IP address to keep the site secure and running.
  • YouTube (Google) provides the films embedded on some pages, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). A film's player loads as you scroll near it, before you press play, so at that point your browser connects to YouTube and Google servers and shares technical details such as your IP address, browser and device information, and that you're visiting this website. YouTube may also store or read data on your device. Pressing play sends YouTube further information about your viewing. YouTube's handling of all of this, both before and after you press play, is covered by Google's privacy policy.

How Long We Keep It

We keep enquiry details only for as long as we need them to respond to you and, if you book with us, to manage your booking and keep our business records. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

Your Rights

You can ask us to show you the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You can also withdraw your consent for us to use it. Email storiesbyabhinavv@gmail.com and we'll respond as soon as we can. If you have a concern about how we've handled your information, write to us at the same address and we'll look into it.

Photos on This Website

The photographs and films on this website are from our real client shoots. If you appear in any of them and would like a photo or film removed, email storiesbyabhinavv@gmail.com and we'll take it down.

All photographs on this website are the copyright of Stories by Abhinav. Please don't copy, download or reuse them without our written permission.

Links to Other Websites

Our pages link to other websites, such as WedMeGood, Instagram and Google reviews. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we aren't responsible for how they handle your information.

Changes to This Policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
