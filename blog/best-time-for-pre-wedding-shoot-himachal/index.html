Best Time for a Pre-Wedding Shoot in Himachal: Month-by-Month

21 November 2026

Home / Blog

Short answer: the best time for a pre-wedding shoot in Himachal Pradesh is October to mid-November, when skies are clearest, the light is golden and the weather is stable. March to May comes a close second for blossoms and green hills, and late December to February is the window for snow. July and August (monsoon) are the riskiest months because of rain and landslides. Here's the full month-by-month picture from a team that shoots in Himachal all year.

Month-by-month guide

January

Peak winter. The best chance of snow at Kufri, Solang, Narkanda and Dalhousie. Days are short and mornings are very cold, so we usually plan one sunrise or midday session rather than a full day. Higher roads such as Rohtang stay closed.

  • Good for: snow shoots, cosy winter looks
  • Watch out for: road closures after heavy snowfall, frozen hands, short golden hour

February

Still snowy at higher places, with slightly longer days. Often the best month for a snow pre-wedding shoot because the snow cover is deep but the worst of the cold has passed.

March

Spring starts. Snow melts in the lower hills, the first blossoms come out in orchards around Shimla, Kullu and Manali, and the light is crisp. A very underrated month with fewer tourists.

April

Apple and cherry blossom season in Kullu and Shimla's orchards. Pleasant temperatures and long days. One of the most beautiful months for orchard and meadow shoots.

May

Warm in the valleys, perfect in the higher hills. Tourist season is in full swing, so popular spots like Solang, the Ridge and Mall Road need very early starts.

June

Clear mornings and greener hills, with pre-monsoon cloud building by afternoon. Roads to Sissu and beyond are usually open. Plan shoots for the morning.

July and August

Monsoon. The hills are at their greenest and the mist can be magical, but heavy rain, landslides and road closures are common, especially on the Mandi–Kullu–Manali highway. If you shoot in monsoon, keep a flexible date and stay close to your base.

September

Monsoon fades out through the month. By late September the air is washed clean and the views are long. A great month if your dates are flexible.

October

Our favourite month. Clear blue skies, warm golden light, autumn colour, comfortable temperatures and snow on the high peaks behind you. It's also the most booked month, so reserve early.

November

Crisp, clear and quieter than October. Mornings are cold but light is beautiful all day. First snowfall on the higher ranges, sometimes at Solang and Narkanda by late November.

December

Cold, clear days in the first half, with the chance of the first proper snowfall in the second half. Christmas and New Year make Shimla and Manali very busy, so avoid the last ten days if you want quiet locations.

Best time by look

  • Snow: late December to February
  • Blossoms: late March to April
  • Lush green: June, and September after the rains
  • Golden autumn: October to mid-November
  • Misty and moody: July and August, if you're flexible on dates

Best time by place

  • Shimla, Kufri, Chail: March–June and September–November; snow from late December (see our Shimla locations guide)
  • Manali, Solang, Naggar: March–June and September–November; snow December–February (see our Manali locations guide)
  • Kasauli: almost all year, but best September–November and March–May (see our Kasauli guide)
  • Dharamshala, Palampur, Bir: October–November and March–April for clear Dhauladhar views (see our Dharamshala guide)
  • Dalhousie and Khajjiar: April–June and September–November (see our Dalhousie guide)
  • Sissu, Lahaul and Spiti: June to early October only, when roads are reliably open (see our Spiti and Lahaul guide)
  • Mandi, Sundernagar, Rewalsar: October to April, avoiding peak summer heat in the valley (see our Mandi guide)

Best time of day

Whatever the month, light matters more than the date. In the mountains we plan around:

  • Sunrise to two hours after: soft light, empty locations, clear peaks before cloud builds
  • Golden hour before sunset: warm, low light that's flattering on skin and outfits
  • Midday: best avoided in open meadows, but fine in forests, old lanes and shaded courtyards

Book early for peak months

October, April and the snow weeks of January–February book out first, both for photographers and for hotels. If you're planning a shoot in one of those windows, try to lock it in two to three months ahead.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best time for a pre-wedding shoot in Himachal?

October to mid-November is the best time, with clear skies and golden light. March to May is a close second for blossoms, and late December to February is best for snow.

Is monsoon a good time for a pre-wedding shoot in Himachal?

July and August are the riskiest months because of heavy rain, landslides and road closures. The mist can look beautiful, but only plan a monsoon shoot with flexible dates.

When can you shoot in Spiti or Sissu?

June to early October is the reliable window, when roads are open. Sissu can sometimes be reached through the Atal Tunnel in winter for snow shoots, depending on conditions.
