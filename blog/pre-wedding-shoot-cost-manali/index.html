Pre-Wedding Shoot Cost in Manali: 2027 Price Guide

21 November 2026

Home / Blog

Short answer: a pre-wedding shoot in Manali usually costs somewhere between ₹15,000 and ₹1,50,000 in total, depending on whether you book a photo-only half day with a local studio or a multi-day photo-and-film shoot with a team that travels in. Most couples land in the middle: a full day of photography at two or three locations, with a short reel added. Below is what actually moves that number, so you can read any quote you get with confidence.

What's included in a Manali pre-wedding shoot price

When photographers quote for a pre-wedding shoot in Manali, the price usually covers:

  • Shooting time — a half day (3–4 hours around golden hour) or a full day (sunrise to sunset, with breaks)
  • Number of locations — Old Manali orchards, Solang Valley, Naggar, Sethan, Jogini Falls and so on
  • Edited photos — delivered in an online gallery, with the number of edits agreed upfront
  • Film add-ons — a 60–90 second reel or a 2–4 minute teaser film, which brings a cinematographer onto the shoot

Things that are often not included, and that you should ask about:

  • Travel and stay for the photographer, if they're coming from outside Himachal
  • Local taxi hire between locations (most couples hire one cab for the day)
  • Entry fees, permits and parking at places like Solang or Hadimba Temple
  • Outfit rental, hair and makeup

Typical price ranges in Manali

These are the broad bands you'll see across studio listings on wedding directories for Manali and Kullu. They're a starting point for comparison, not a quote:

  • Local photo-only half day: roughly ₹10,000–₹25,000
  • Full-day photo shoot, two or three locations: roughly ₹25,000–₹60,000
  • Photo + reel or teaser film: roughly ₹50,000–₹1,20,000
  • Multi-day, multi-location shoot with a travelling team: ₹1,00,000 and upwards, plus travel

The five things that change the cost most

1. Where your photographer is based

This is the biggest hidden cost. A team flying in from Delhi or Mumbai adds flights or a long drive, hotel nights and often an extra day either side. A Himachal-based team drives up on the day or the evening before. Our studio is in Sundernagar, a few hours' drive from Manali, so for most Manali shoots there's no flight in the quote.

2. Season

Manali has three peak windows for pre-wedding shoots: March–May (apple blossoms), October (autumn colour and clear skies) and December–February (snow). Hotels cost more in these months and good photographers book up early. Shooting in early June or late November can be noticeably cheaper overall, with very good light.

3. Snow shoots need buffer days

A snow pre-wedding shoot in Manali is something many couples dream of, but snowfall is unpredictable and roads beyond Solang can close. A sensible snow shoot plan keeps one flexible backup day, and that extra day affects both the photographer's fee and your hotel bill. Read our snow wedding and winter shoot guide before booking a winter date.

4. How far up you go

Old Manali, Vashisht and the orchards are minutes apart. Sethan needs a steep drive and a short walk. Sissu and Atal Tunnel are a longer day trip (see our Spiti and Lahaul shoot guide). Every extra hour on the road is an hour not shooting, so longer routes usually mean a full-day booking.

5. Film

Adding a reel or teaser film adds a second person, more equipment and several days of editing. If you mainly want something for Instagram, a short vertical reel is the best value. If you want a film to play at the sangeet or reception, budget for a proper teaser.

A sample one-day Manali shoot plan

  1. Sunrise (6:00–8:30): Solang Valley meadows, before the crowds and before cloud builds over the peaks
  2. Breakfast and outfit change
  3. Late morning (10:30–12:00): Naggar Castle and the wooden lanes around it, which work well in softer, indirect light
  4. Lunch break and rest
  5. Golden hour (16:00–18:00): Old Manali apple orchards along the Manalsu river

That's three looks, three backdrops and one taxi for the day.

How to get an accurate quote

Send your photographer:

  • Your preferred dates, plus one backup date
  • The locations you have in mind (or ask them to suggest some)
  • Whether you want photos only, or a reel or film too
  • How many outfit changes you're planning

With those four answers any good photographer can give you a clear, itemised price instead of a vague rate card.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a pre-wedding shoot in Manali cost?

Most pre-wedding shoots in Manali cost between ₹15,000 and ₹1,50,000 in total. A local photo-only half day sits at the lower end; a full day with a reel or teaser film, or a multi-day shoot with a travelling team, sits at the higher end.

What is the best month for a pre-wedding shoot in Manali?

October is the most reliable month for clear skies and golden light. March to May brings apple blossoms, and December to February is the window for snow at Solang Valley.

Are travel and permits included in a Manali pre-wedding package?

Often not. Ask whether the quote covers the photographer's travel and stay, local taxi hire between locations, and entry or permit fees at places like Solang or Hadimba Temple.
