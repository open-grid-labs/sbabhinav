Most couples planning a pre-wedding shoot in Himachal think of Manali or Shimla first. But Mandi district, right in the centre of the state, has lakes, temple towns, river valleys and high meadows that are just as beautiful and far less crowded. It's also our home district: Stories by Abhinav is based in Sundernagar, so these are the places we know best. Here are our favourite pre-wedding shoot locations in Mandi and Sundernagar, with the best time to visit each one.

## Sundernagar Lake

The lake in Sundernagar town is calm, open and easy to reach, with wide skies and hills around it. It's ideal for a relaxed evening session or a first short shoot for couples who are nervous in front of the camera.

- **Best light:** the hour before sunset, with reflections on the water
- **Best season:** October to March, when the valley is cooler and the air is clear
- **Good for:** couples who want a simple, local shoot without a long drive

## Mandi town and the Beas riverfront

Mandi is called the "Chhoti Kashi" of Himachal for its many old stone temples. The temple-lined lanes, the ghats along the Beas and the old bridges give you a heritage look that's completely different from a hill station.

- **Best light:** early morning, when the riverfront is quiet and the light is soft
- **Etiquette:** temples are active places of worship. We shoot respectfully from outside, avoid prayer times and ask before photographing inside any premises.
- **Good for:** traditional outfits, sarees and kurta sets

## Rewalsar Lake (Tso Pema)

About an hour from Mandi, Rewalsar is a sacred lake town for Hindus, Buddhists and Sikhs, with monasteries, prayer flags and a giant Padmasambhava statue on the hillside above. The colours and the calm make it one of the most distinctive locations in Himachal.

- **Best light:** morning, or late afternoon from the hillside looking down on the lake
- **Etiquette:** it's a pilgrimage site, so we keep it low-key, dress modestly near the monasteries and never block worshippers
- **Good for:** couples who want something different from the usual hill-station look

## Prashar Lake

Prashar is a high-altitude lake at around 2,700 metres, with the pagoda-style Prashar Rishi temple on its shore and wide meadows all around. On a clear day you can see the Dhauladhar, Pir Panjal and Kinnaur ranges. It's one of the most breathtaking places in Himachal for a shoot.

- **Best light:** sunrise or the last hour before sunset
- **Best season:** April to June and September to November. In winter it's often snowbound, which is beautiful but harder to reach.
- **Getting there:** about 2–2.5 hours from Mandi by road, followed by a short walk
- **Good for:** big landscape frames, flowing lehengas and gowns

## Barot Valley

Barot is a quiet valley with the Uhl river, trout streams, deodar forest and old wooden houses. It's about three hours from Mandi and feels completely off the tourist map.

- **Best light:** golden hour in the forest
- **Best season:** April to June and September to November
- **Good for:** a nature-heavy, uncrowded shoot. Pair it with an overnight stay.

## Janjehli and Shikari Devi

High up in Seraj, Janjehli valley has meadows, forests and apple orchards. The Shikari Devi temple above it sits on a ridge with sweeping views. This is one of the most untouched corners of Himachal.

- **Best season:** May to June and September to October. Roads to Shikari Devi close in winter.
- **Good for:** couples who want a remote, adventurous feel

## Kamrunag

Kamrunag is a sacred lake and temple reached by a forest trek from Rohanda, near Sundernagar. It's beautiful, but it's a proper hike of a few hours each way, so it only suits couples happy to trek in comfortable clothes and change at the top.

## A one-day plan from Sundernagar

1. **Sunrise:** Rewalsar Lake, with the monasteries and the lake still quiet
2. **Late morning:** Mandi's temple lanes and the Beas riverfront in traditional outfits
3. **Afternoon:** rest and outfit change
4. **Golden hour:** Sundernagar Lake

For a two-day shoot, add Prashar Lake for sunrise on day two, or Barot or Janjehli for a forest and meadow session.

## Why shoot in Mandi rather than Manali?

- **Fewer crowds.** You won't be waiting for tourists to move out of your frame.
- **Lower cost.** No long drive or overnight stay for a Sundernagar-based team means a leaner quote.
- **More variety in a small area.** Lakes, temples, river, forest and high meadows within a couple of hours.
- **Easy to combine.** Mandi sits on the highway to Kullu and Manali, so you can do a Mandi day and a Manali day back to back.
