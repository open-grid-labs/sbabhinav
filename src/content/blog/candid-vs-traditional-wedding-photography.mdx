Wedding photographer se baat karte hi ek sawaal zaroor aata hai: "Aapko candid chahiye ya traditional?" Kayi couples ko lagta hai ki ek chunna padega. Asal mein zyada tar achhi weddings mein dono ka mix hota hai. Yahan simple bhasha mein samjhaate hain ki dono mein farak kya hai, kaunsa kab kaam aata hai, aur aapko kya poochna chahiye.

## Traditional wedding photography kya hai?

Traditional photography ka matlab hai **planned, posed photos**: couple ke saath har family member ki group photo, stage pe photos, rituals ke important moments jahan sab camera ki taraf dekhte hain.

- **Faayda:** koi bhi rishtedaar chhootta nahi. Dadi, nani, mama, bua, sabki photo album mein aati hai.
- **Kab zaroori hai:** jab family badi ho, aur buzurg chahte hain ki "sabki photo aani chahiye"
- **Kami:** photos stiff lag sakti hain, aur expressions naturally nahi aate

## Candid wedding photography kya hai?

Candid ka matlab hai **asli moments ko bina rok-tok capture karna**: vidaai pe maa ki aankhein, haldi mein bhai-behen ki masti, pheron ke beech dulhan ki muskaan. Photographer dur se, chupchap kaam karta hai.

- **Faayda:** photos mein emotion aur kahani hoti hai. Saalon baad bhi wo pal yaad aata hai.
- **Kab best hai:** haldi, mehendi, sangeet, baraat, vidaai, jahan bahut kuch ek saath ho raha hota hai
- **Kami:** sirf candid pe rely karein toh kuch formal family photos chhoot sakti hain

## Cinematic photography aur films

Aajkal "cinematic" shabd bhi bahut sunne ko milta hai. Iska matlab hai couple portraits aur moments ko film jaisi lighting, framing aur editing ke saath shoot karna, aur saath mein ek **highlight film** ya **teaser** banana. Pahadon mein ye style bahut achha lagta hai, kyunki mountains khud ek cinematic backdrop hain. Dekhein humari [wedding films](/services/wedding-films/) service.

## Toh aapko kya chunna chahiye?

Humari salaah: **dono ka mix**, aur zyada focus candid pe.

- **Rituals aur emotional moments:** candid
- **Family groups:** ek fixed time pe, ek list ke saath, jaldi aur organised traditional photos
- **Couple portraits:** golden hour mein 20–30 minute ka ek chhota session, natural poses ke saath

Is tarah aapko family ki har photo bhi milti hai aur din ki asli kahani bhi.

## Photographer se ye sawaal zaroor poochein

1. Aapka style zyada candid hai ya traditional? Kya poori wedding ki gallery dikha sakte hain, sirf best photos nahi?
2. Family group photos ka kya plan hota hai? Kya aap list ke hisaab se kaam karte hain?
3. Ek function pe kitne photographers aayenge? Candid aur traditional dono ek saath cover ho paayenge?
4. Kitni edited photos milengi, aur kitne time mein?
5. Album aur film included hai, ya alag se?

## Himachali weddings mein kya kaam karta hai?

Himachali weddings aksar family-centred hoti hain, jahan mama ka role, dham, Nati aur purane rituals sab important hain. Yahan **candid photography** bahut achhi lagti hai, kyunki asli emotions har jagah hote hain. Par buzurgon ke liye **traditional group photos** bhi utni hi zaroori hain. Isliye hum dono karte hain.
