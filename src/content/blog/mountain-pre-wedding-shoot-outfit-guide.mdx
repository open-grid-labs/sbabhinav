Himachal mein pre-wedding shoot ke liye outfit choose karna plains ke shoot se thoda alag hai — light, temperature aur terrain sab different hote hain. Yahan kuch practical guidelines hain jo hum couples ko shoot se pehle dete hain.

## Layering zaroori hai

Mountain weather din bhar mein badal sakta hai — subah thandi, dopahar garam, aur shaam phir thandi. Ek hi heavy outfit ke bharose mat rahiye. Light inner layers ke saath ek achha-looking outer layer (shrug, cape, ya structured jacket) carry karein jise aap easily on/off kar sakein bina look kharab kiye.

## Fabric weight matter karta hai

Bahut halka, flowy fabric (jaise pure chiffon) hawa mein control karna mushkil ho jaata hai — khaas kar open meadows ya ridges pe jahan wind zyada hoti hai. Thoda structured fabric (georgette, silk blends, ya heavier cotton) better drape deta hai aur wind mein bhi manageable rehta hai.

## Colours jo background ke against pop karein

- **Green backdrops** (orchards, forests): jewel tones — maroon, mustard, deep blue — achhe se stand out karte hain
- **Snow / white backdrops**: bold colours (red, emerald, royal blue) dramatic contrast dete hain; pastels snow ke against thode wash out ho sakte hain
- **Golden-hour open valleys**: warm tones (rust, terracotta, gold) light ke saath naturally blend karte hain

Bahut zyada busy prints avoid karein — mountain backdrops already visually rich hote hain, simple silhouettes aur solid ya subtle-print outfits better kaam karte hain.

## Footwear practical rakhein

Zyada tar locations (orchards, meadows, riverbanks) uneven ground pe hote hain. Heels carry karein shoot ke liye, par walk karne ke liye comfortable flats ya boots pehnein. Hum aksar location pe pahunch kar hi heels change karne ki suggest karte hain.

## Accessories halka rakhein

Heavy jewellery ya bade accessories wind aur movement ke saath distracting ho sakte hain candid, movement-based shots mein. Simple, lightweight pieces choose karein jo aapko move karne se na roken.

## Do outfits carry karein

Agar budget aur time allow kare, do outfits plan karein — ek zyada traditional/festive backdrop ke liye, ek casual/flowy look open landscapes ke liye. Isse gallery mein variety bhi aati hai.
