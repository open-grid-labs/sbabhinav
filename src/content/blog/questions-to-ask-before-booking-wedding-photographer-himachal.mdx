Wedding photographer book karna ek bada decision hai — aur ek baar wedding day nikal jaaye, dobara mauka nahi milta. Booking se pehle ye 10 sawaal zaroor poochein, chahe aap humse shoot karayein ya kisi aur se.

## 1. Aap kitne functions/days cover karte hain?

Confirm karein ki quote mein exactly kaunse functions (mehendi, haldi, sangeet, wedding, reception) included hain aur kaunse extra hain.

## 2. Team mein kitne log honge?

Ek photographer se do-photographer-plus-cinematographer team tak — team size seedha coverage quality aur price dono ko affect karta hai, khaas kar jab events overlap hote hain.

## 3. Kya aapne Himachal mein pehle shoot kiya hai?

Mountain light, hill-station venues aur local logistics ka experience matter karta hai. Ek photographer jo yahan pehle shoot kar chuka hai, weather aur venue challenges better handle karta hai.

## 4. Delivery timeline kya hai?

Edited photos aur film milne mein kitna time lagega — 2 hafte, 2 mahine? Ye likhwa lein taaki expectations clear rahein.

## 5. Kitni photos deliver hoti hain?

Raw count nahi, edited aur curated gallery ka approximate number poochein — aur kya woh high-resolution files hain ya sirf web-sized.

## 6. Raw files milte hain ya nahi?

Kuch photographers raw/unedited files nahi dete — sirf final edited set. Agar aapko raw files chahiye, ye advance mein confirm kar lein.

## 7. Travel aur stay charges included hain ya alag?

Destination wedding ke liye travel, accommodation aur food charges quote mein included hain ya separately bill honge — ye upfront clarify karein.

## 8. Drone coverage available hai?

Agar aapko aerial shots chahiye Himachal ke landscapes ke liye, confirm karein ki drone included hai, add-on hai, ya team ke paas licensed operator hai ya nahi.

## 9. Booking confirm karne ke liye deposit kitna hai, aur cancellation policy kya hai?

Advance amount, refund policy, aur date-change conditions — sab likhwa lein taaki koi confusion na ho agar plans badalte hain.

## 10. Backup plan kya hai agar photographer khud available na ho?

Illness, emergency ya double-booking jaisi situations mein studio ka backup arrangement kya hai — ek replacement shooter, ya poora refund?

Ye sawaal poochna kisi bhi genuine studio ko awkward nahi lagega — jawab dena unki professionalism dikhata hai.
