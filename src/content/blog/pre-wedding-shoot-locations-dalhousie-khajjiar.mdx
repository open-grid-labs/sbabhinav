Dalhousie, Khajjiar and Chamba sit in the far north-west of Himachal and get a fraction of the crowds of Shimla and Manali. You get colonial churches, deep pine and deodar forest, wide green meadows and the old royal town of Chamba on the Ravi river, all within about an hour of each other. Here's our guide to the best pre-wedding shoot locations in Dalhousie, Khajjiar and Chamba.

## Dalhousie

### Gandhi Chowk and Mall Road

The centre of Dalhousie has a calm, old-world feel, with colonial buildings and churches around the square. Early mornings are the quietest.

### St. John's Church and St. Francis Church

Dalhousie's historic stone churches are among the oldest in the region and make a beautiful, timeless backdrop. They're places of worship, so we shoot outside and ask before photographing anything else.

### Bakrota Hills and the upper Mall

A quiet road around Bakrota with forest on one side and valley views on the other. It's one of the best places in Dalhousie for walking shots and natural light portraits.

- **Best light:** late afternoon through the trees

### Panchpula

A small waterfall and streams in the forest just outside town. Good for a few fresh, green frames in the morning before visitors arrive.

## Khajjiar

Khajjiar is often called the "mini Switzerland of India": a wide, saucer-shaped meadow surrounded by tall deodar forest, with a small lake and the Khajji Nag temple. It's about 45 minutes to an hour from Dalhousie.

- **Best light:** early morning, when the meadow is empty and mist sits in the trees, or the last hour before sunset
- **Best season:** April to June for green, September to November for clear skies, December to February for snow
- **Crowd tip:** by late morning the meadow fills with tourists, ponies and zorbing. Arrive at sunrise, or walk into the forest edge for clean backgrounds.
- **Good for:** flowing lehengas, gowns and wide romantic frames

## Kalatop Wildlife Sanctuary

On the road between Dalhousie and Khajjiar, Kalatop is a dense forest of tall trees and quiet trails. It's a protected area with entry rules and fees, so check the current conditions before you plan your shoot. The forest light is beautiful for intimate portraits.

## Chamba

Chamba is a thousand-year-old royal town on the banks of the Ravi, about 1.5–2 hours from Dalhousie. It has a character completely different from a hill station:

- **Chaugan:** the large open green in the centre of town, surrounded by old buildings
- **Lakshmi Narayan temple complex:** beautiful old stone shikhara temples (shoot respectfully, from outside, and avoid prayer times)
- **Rang Mahal and old palace areas:** heritage architecture with lots of texture
- **Ravi riverbanks:** open, wide frames with hills rising behind

Chamba suits couples who want heritage, culture and a traditional look. Chamba rumal embroidery and local jewellery can make beautiful details for an outfit.

## Best time for a shoot in Dalhousie and Khajjiar

- **April to June:** green meadows and pleasant days. Our pick for first-timers.
- **September to November:** clear skies and long views
- **December to February:** snow is likely in Dalhousie and Khajjiar, with some road disruption
- **July and August:** very wet and foggy, with landslide risk on the roads

## How to plan a Dalhousie shoot

Dalhousie is far from most cities: about 2.5–3 hours from Pathankot, the nearest railway hub and airport. We recommend:

- **Two days on location:** Khajjiar at sunrise on day one, Dalhousie in the afternoon; Chamba or Kalatop on day two
- **Staying in Khajjiar or Dalhousie,** so sunrise is easy
