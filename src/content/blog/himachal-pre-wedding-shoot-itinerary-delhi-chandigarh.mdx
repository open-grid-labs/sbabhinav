Delhi ya Chandigarh se Himachal mein pre-wedding shoot plan kar rahe hain, par sirf 2-3 din ki chhutti hai? Achhi baat ye hai ki itne time mein bhi bahut khoobsurat shoot ho sakti hai, bas route sahi chunna hai. Yahan teen practical itineraries hain, distance aur timing ke saath, taaki aapka zyada time shoot mein jaaye aur kam gaadi mein.

## Pehle distance samajh lijiye

Chandigarh se road ka approximate time (traffic aur weather ke hisaab se badal sakta hai):

- **Kasauli:** 1.5–2 ghante
- **Shimla:** 3–4 ghante
- **Chail:** 3–3.5 ghante
- **Sundernagar / Mandi:** 4–5 ghante
- **Dharamshala:** 5–6 ghante
- **Manali:** 8–9 ghante

Delhi se inme lagbhag 5–6 ghante aur jod lijiye. Shimla, Kullu (Bhuntar) aur Kangra (Gaggal) mein chhote airports bhi hain, par flights weather pe depend karti hain, isliye backup plan rakhein.

## Itinerary 1: Kasauli + Chail (2 din, sabse aasan)

Kam travel, zyada shoot. Chandigarh walon ke liye best.

- **Din 1:** subah jaldi nikalein, sunrise tak Kasauli. Mall Road aur Christ Church pe sunrise session. Dopahar mein heritage cottage ya hotel lawn. Shaam ko Gilbert Trail aur Sunset Point.
- **Din 2:** Chail ki taraf. Subah deodar forest, phir Chail Palace (permission ke saath), golden hour mein cricket ground ke aas-paas. Raat tak wapas Chandigarh.

Details ke liye humara [Kasauli locations guide](/blog/pre-wedding-shoot-locations-kasauli/) padhein.

## Itinerary 2: Shimla (2–3 din, classic)

Colonial heritage + forest + snow (winter mein).

- **Din 1:** Shimla pahunchein, aaram karein. Shaam ko Jakhoo ke deodar trail pe golden hour.
- **Din 2:** sunrise pe The Ridge, Mall Road aur Christ Church (bheed se pehle). Outfit change. Dopahar ke baad Mashobra ke orchards ya Naldehra.
- **Din 3 (optional):** Kufri ya Narkanda. Winter mein snow shoot, garmiyon mein meadows. Phir wapsi.

Poori list humare [Shimla pre-wedding locations guide](/blog/best-pre-wedding-shoot-locations-shimla/) mein hai.

## Itinerary 3: Mandi + Manali (3–4 din, sabse variety)

Agar aapko lakes, temples, orchards aur snow peaks sab chahiye.

- **Din 1:** Chandigarh se Sundernagar/Mandi. Shaam ko Sundernagar Lake pe golden hour.
- **Din 2:** sunrise pe Rewalsar Lake, phir Mandi ke temple lanes aur Beas ghat. Dopahar mein Manali ke liye nikalein.
- **Din 3:** sunrise pe Solang Valley, dopahar mein Naggar Castle, golden hour mein Old Manali ke apple orchards.
- **Din 4 (optional):** Atal Tunnel paar karke Sissu, phir wapsi.

Dekhein humara [Mandi aur Sundernagar locations guide](/blog/pre-wedding-shoot-locations-mandi-sundernagar/) aur [Manali locations guide](/blog/best-pre-wedding-shoot-locations-manali/).

## Bonus: Dharamshala + Palampur (2–3 din)

Dhauladhar ke snow peaks, monasteries aur tea gardens. Din 1 McLeodganj aur Naddi, din 2 Palampur tea gardens aur Bir. Poora plan humare [Dharamshala guide](/blog/pre-wedding-shoot-locations-dharamshala-mcleodganj/) mein hai.

## Planning ke kuch zaroori tips

- **Raat ko drive mat kijiye.** Pahadi roads pe raat ki driving thakaati bhi hai aur risky bhi. Pehle din aaram karein, taaki shoot wale din fresh dikhein.
- **Sunrise aur golden hour ke around plan karein.** Dopahar ki dhoop mountains mein harsh hoti hai. Us time travel ya outfit change karein.
- **Ek cab poore din ke liye book karein.** Locations ke beech jaldi move ho paayenge aur outfits gaadi mein safe rahenge.
- **Ek backup din rakhein,** khaas kar winter (snow) aur monsoon (landslides) mein.
- **Outfits 3–4 se zyada mat rakhiye.** Har change mein 20–30 minute lagte hain.
- **Weekend avoid karein** agar ho sake. Shimla, Kasauli aur Manali weekends pe bahut crowded hote hain.
