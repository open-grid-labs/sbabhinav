Dharamshala has something no other hill town in Himachal has in quite the same way: the snow-covered Dhauladhar range rising straight up behind the town, visible from almost everywhere. Add McLeodganj's monasteries and cafés, the tea gardens of Kangra and Palampur, and the paragliding slopes of Bir, and you have one of the most varied regions in the state for a pre-wedding shoot. Here are our favourite locations.

## McLeodganj

### Bhagsu Nag and the waterfall trail

The Bhagsunag temple and the short walk towards Bhagsu waterfall give you mountain slopes, stone steps and water. The trail gets busy by mid-morning, so we go early.

### Tsuglagkhang complex and the monastery lanes

McLeodganj is home to the Dalai Lama's temple complex, and its streets are full of prayer flags, prayer wheels and colourful monastery buildings. These are active religious places, so we keep our shoot discreet, never photograph monks or worshippers without permission, and stay out of prayer halls.

### St. John in the Wilderness

A 19th-century stone church set in deodar forest between McLeodganj and Forsyth Ganj. The church, its stained glass and the quiet graveyard trees make a moody, timeless backdrop. Shoot respectfully, outside the church.

### Naddi viewpoint

A little above McLeodganj, Naddi has one of the best close-up views of the Dhauladhar. It's our favourite sunrise or sunset spot in the area.

## Dharamshala

### Dharamshala Cricket Stadium

The HPCA stadium is famous for having the Dhauladhar behind its pavilion. Shooting inside needs permission from the stadium management, but the views of the stadium and the mountains from nearby roads are beautiful too.

### Dal Lake and Naddi road

Not the Kashmir one. A small lake surrounded by deodar trees on the way to Naddi. Calm, green and good for a quick stop.

### Tea gardens around Dharamshala

Dharamshala and Kangra have tea estates where the rows of green bushes lead your eye up to the snow peaks. Many are private, so ask the estate before walking in.

## Palampur

About an hour from Dharamshala, Palampur is the tea capital of north India. Its rolling tea gardens with the Dhauladhar behind them are among the most photogenic places in Himachal.

- **Best light:** golden hour, with low light across the tea rows
- **Best season:** March to May, when the new leaves are fresh green, and October to November for clear mountain views
- **Good for:** sarees, flowing dupattas and soft pastel outfits
- **Nearby:** Andretta artists' village and the old stone temples at Baijnath

## Bir Billing

Bir is famous as one of the best paragliding sites in the world, with Billing as the take-off point high above. For a pre-wedding shoot it offers:

- **Tibetan colony and monasteries in Bir,** with bright colours and prayer flags
- **Tea gardens around Bir,** quieter than Palampur
- **Open hillsides at Billing,** with huge views over the Kangra valley at sunset
- **Paragliding shots:** some couples include a tandem flight. We plan this with licensed operators only, and only when the weather allows.

## Best time for a pre-wedding shoot in Dharamshala

- **October to mid-November:** clear, crisp skies and the sharpest views of the Dhauladhar. Our pick.
- **March to May:** tea gardens in fresh leaf, rhododendrons in bloom, snow still on the peaks
- **December to February:** snow on the Dhauladhar, cold mornings, occasional snow in McLeodganj
- **July to September:** Dharamshala is one of the wettest places in India in monsoon. Avoid it unless you love the mist.

## A two-day Kangra valley plan

1. **Day one, sunrise:** Naddi viewpoint
2. **Day one, late morning:** St. John in the Wilderness and the McLeodganj lanes
3. **Day one, golden hour:** a tea garden near Dharamshala
4. **Day two, morning:** Palampur tea gardens
5. **Day two, golden hour:** Bir monasteries, then sunset at Billing
