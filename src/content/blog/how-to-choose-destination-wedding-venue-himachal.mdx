Choosing a wedding venue in the mountains isn't like choosing a banquet hall in the city. Access roads, weather, rooms for guests, power backup and even where the sun sets all matter more in Himachal than anywhere else. Here's the checklist we wish every couple used before signing a contract, from a photography team that has worked at hotel lawns, resorts, family homes and temple courtyards across Himachal.

## 1. Can your guests actually get there?

- **Distance from Chandigarh:** Kasauli is about 1.5 hours away, Shimla 3–4, Dharamshala 5–6, Manali 8–9. Older relatives feel every hour.
- **Last stretch of road:** some of the most beautiful venues sit at the end of a narrow, steep road. Can a bus or tempo traveller get up it? Is it safe at night?
- **Winter and monsoon access:** ask what happens after heavy snow or rain. Is there a plowed road? A backup route?
- **Nearest airport and railway station:** Shimla, Kullu–Bhuntar and Kangra–Gaggal have small airports, but flights are weather-dependent. Chandigarh is the reliable fallback.

## 2. Is there a weather backup for every function?

Mountain weather can change in an hour. For every outdoor function, ask:

- Is there an **indoor hall or a proper waterproof tent**, and does it fit your full guest list?
- Who decides when to switch, and how quickly can decor move?
- Is the backup space included, or extra?

## 3. Rooms and room blocks

At a destination wedding, rooms are usually the biggest cost. Ask:

- How many rooms are there, and is a **full buyout** required?
- What's the **minimum guarantee** on rooms and plates?
- Where do overflow guests stay, and how far is it?
- What are check-in and check-out times for a multi-day wedding?

See our [destination wedding cost guide](/blog/destination-wedding-cost-himachal-pradesh/) for how venue costs fit into the full budget.

## 4. Power, heating and essentials

- **Power backup:** hill areas see power cuts. Is there a generator big enough for lights, sound and the kitchen?
- **Heating:** for winter weddings, are rooms and indoor spaces properly heated?
- **Water and washrooms** near the function areas, especially for outdoor events
- **Mobile network and Wi-Fi:** matters for coordination and for guests

## 5. Noise and time rules

Many hill venues have rules on music after a certain hour, and some areas have local restrictions. Check:

- What time music must stop outdoors
- Whether a late-night pheras muhurat is allowed, and where
- Whether fireworks or sky lanterns are allowed (often they're not, for forest-fire safety)

## 6. Outside vendors

- Can you bring your own **caterer, decorator and photographer**, or are there tie-ups?
- Is there an extra fee for outside vendors?
- Does the venue allow a traditional **dham cooked by local botis**?

## 7. Photography and light (our checklist)

This is where we can help you most. When you visit a venue, check:

- **Where does the sun set?** A lawn facing west gives you golden-hour light behind the mandap. A lawn in the shadow of a hill loses its light by 4 pm in winter.
- **Where's the view?** Can the mandap be set up with the mountains behind it rather than a wall?
- **Indoor light:** does the banquet hall have windows, or will everything be artificial light?
- **Getting-ready rooms:** a bright room with a window and some space makes a big difference to bride and groom portraits.
- **Couple portrait spots:** is there a garden, orchard, forest path or viewpoint within a few minutes' walk?
- **Drone policy:** is flying allowed? Is the venue near an airport? See our [permits and drone guide](/blog/pre-wedding-shoot-permits-drone-rules-himachal/).

## 8. Season and pricing

- **Peak seasons:** May–June, October–November and the Christmas–New Year week are the busiest and the most expensive
- **Shoulder seasons:** March–April and late September give good weather at better rates
- **Monsoon:** July–August is cheapest, but landslides and road closures make it risky for guests

Our [best time guide](/blog/best-time-for-pre-wedding-shoot-himachal/) breaks this down month by month.

## 9. Contract details

Before you sign, get these in writing:

- Total cost with taxes, and the payment schedule
- Cancellation and rescheduling policy, especially for weather or road closures
- What happens if the venue double-books or changes ownership
- Exactly which spaces are yours, and for how long

## Which region suits which wedding?

- **Big guest list, easy access:** Shimla or Kasauli
- **Intimate and dramatic:** Manali or Sissu
- **Calm, cultural, mountain views:** Dharamshala or Palampur
- **Close to home for Himachali families:** Mandi, Sundernagar and the Beas valley
