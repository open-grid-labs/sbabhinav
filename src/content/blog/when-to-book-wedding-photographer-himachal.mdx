**Short answer:** book your wedding photographer **6 to 9 months before** a peak-season wedding in Himachal (October–December and February–March, around the main muhurat dates), and **3 to 6 months before** an off-season one. For a pre-wedding shoot, **2 to 3 months** ahead is usually enough, except for October and snow-season dates, which fill up faster. Here's why timing matters, and what to do at each stage.

## Why good photographers book out early

- **Muhurat dates cluster.** Hindu wedding dates are set by auspicious muhurats, so many weddings fall on the same few dates each season. A photographer can only be at one wedding per date.
- **Peak seasons overlap.** In Himachal, wedding season, tourist season and pre-wedding season all peak around October–November and again in spring.
- **Multi-day weddings take more dates.** A three-day destination wedding takes three days out of a photographer's calendar, plus travel.

## Wedding seasons in Himachal

- **Peak:** October to mid-December, and late January to March. Most local and destination weddings happen here.
- **Summer:** April to June. Popular for destination weddings in higher places like Manali and Shimla, where it's cool.
- **Quiet:** July to September (monsoon) and some weeks with few muhurats. Easier to book, but plan around rain.

## A booking timeline

### 9–12 months before

- Fix your wedding dates and shortlist venues
- Shortlist photographers by looking at **full wedding galleries**, not just Instagram highlights
- For a destination wedding, check your photographer's availability before you sign the venue

### 6–9 months before

- Talk to your shortlisted photographers, ask for itemised quotes (our [cost guide](/blog/wedding-photography-cost-himachal-pradesh-guide/) shows what to ask for)
- Book your photographer with a signed contract and deposit
- Decide whether you want a film, a drone and a pre-wedding shoot

### 3–6 months before

- Plan your **pre-wedding shoot** dates and locations. See our [best time for a pre-wedding shoot](/blog/best-time-for-pre-wedding-shoot-himachal/) guide.
- Share your guest list highlights and family traditions with your photographer

### 1–2 months before

- Share the final schedule of every function, including the muhurat time
- Send a list of must-have family group photos
- Share venue details, contact people and any permissions needed

### 1–2 weeks before

- Final call to confirm timings, addresses, the key people and the shot list
- Confirm weather backups for outdoor functions

## Booking at short notice

It's still possible to find a good photographer at short notice, especially for:

- **Off-season dates** and weekdays
- **Smaller, single-day weddings**
- **Local weddings,** where no travel is needed

## Before you pay a deposit

- Is the date confirmed in writing?
- What happens if you need to change the date?
- What's the cancellation policy?
- Who exactly will shoot your wedding: the lead photographer, or another team member?
- When will you receive your photos and film?
