Kasauli is the closest hill station to Chandigarh, only about 1.5 hours by road, which makes it one of the easiest places for a pre-wedding shoot in Himachal. In one day you get pine forests, colonial cottages, quiet churches and wide valley views, without the long drive to Shimla or Manali. Here are the best pre-wedding shoot locations in Kasauli and nearby, with tips on light, season and permissions.

## Mall Road and the Lower Mall

Kasauli's Mall Road is small and charming, lined with old shops, stone walls and colonial-era buildings. It's much calmer than Shimla's Mall Road, especially on weekday mornings.

- **Best light:** early morning, before the shops open
- **Good for:** candid walking shots, vintage looks, western outfits

## Christ Church, Kasauli

Built in the mid-1800s, this small Gothic-style church with its stained glass and stone walls is one of the prettiest buildings in Kasauli. It's a place of worship, so we photograph the exterior and the surroundings, and ask permission before anything else.

## Sunset Point (Hawa Ghar)

On the Upper Mall, the sunset point looks out over the plains and the valley. On a clear evening you can see the lights of Chandigarh come on below.

- **Best light:** the half hour before sunset
- **Good for:** silhouettes and wide, romantic frames

## Gilbert Trail

A short, quiet trail through pine and oak forest along the edge of the hill, with steep drops and long views. It's one of the best places in Kasauli for a natural, unposed forest look.

- **Best light:** late afternoon, as the sun filters through the pines
- **Tip:** wear flat shoes for the walk and change at the spot

## Monkey Point (Manki Point)

The highest point in Kasauli, with a Hanuman temple at the top and sweeping views. It falls inside an Air Force area, so there are entry rules, ID checks and restrictions on photography, and cameras may not be allowed at all. Check the current rules before you plan your shoot around it. We usually treat it as a sightseeing stop rather than a shoot location.

## Timber Trail and Parwanoo

On the way up from Chandigarh, the area around Parwanoo has forested slopes and long views over the Shivalik hills. It's a good first stop for couples driving up, before the light gets harsh.

## Heritage hotels and private cottages

Some of the best pre-wedding backdrops in Kasauli are private: old colonial cottages, heritage hotels with lawns, and boutique stays with wooden verandas. Many allow shoots for guests or for a fee. Staying at one also makes a sunrise session easy.

## Nearby: Dagshai, Barog and Chail

- **Dagshai:** one of the oldest cantonment towns in the region, with old stone buildings and quiet roads. Parts are restricted, so check before shooting.
- **Barog:** pine forests and the famous Barog railway station on the Kalka–Shimla heritage line
- **Chail:** about two hours away, with Chail Palace, deodar forest and the old cricket ground. It's best as its own half-day (see our [Shimla locations guide](/blog/best-pre-wedding-shoot-locations-shimla/))

## Best time for a pre-wedding shoot in Kasauli

- **September to November:** clear skies and the best views. Our pick.
- **March to May:** pleasant and green, with blossoms in early spring
- **December to February:** cold and crisp. Snow is occasional in Kasauli itself, and never guaranteed.
- **July and August:** foggy and wet, but beautiful if you're flexible

## A simple one-day Kasauli plan

1. **Leave Chandigarh at about 5:00** and reach Kasauli by sunrise
2. **Sunrise:** Mall Road and Christ Church, while it's empty
3. **Late morning:** a heritage cottage or hotel lawn
4. **Afternoon:** rest, outfit change, lunch
5. **Golden hour:** Gilbert Trail, then Sunset Point

## Who Kasauli suits best

Kasauli is ideal for couples from Chandigarh, Punjab, Haryana and Delhi who want a mountain look without taking several days off.
