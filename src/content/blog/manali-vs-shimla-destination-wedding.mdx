Himachal Pradesh mein destination wedding plan karne wale zyada tar couples do options ke beech confuse hote hain — Manali ya Shimla. Dono hill stations hain, dono ke paas mountain views hain, par experience kaafi alag hai. Yahan ek honest comparison hai, taaki decision aasan ho.

## Venue ka character

Manali ka vibe zyada rugged aur alpine hai — pine forests, snow-line ke close venues, aur orchards. Yahan ke venues aksar valley ya river ke saamne hote hain, open-air setups ke saath.

Shimla zyada colonial-era architecture aur organised infrastructure offer karta hai — heritage buildings, well-established banquet halls, aur The Ridge jaisi central locations. Agar aapko ek "old Himachal" charm chahiye jo thoda zyada structured ho, Shimla us direction mein jhukta hai.

## Weather window

- **Manali**: March-June aur September-November sabse reliable hain. Peak winter (December-February) mein snow shoots ke liye achha hai, par kuch higher venues access mushkil ho sakte hain.
- **Shimla**: Thoda zyada moderate climate saal bhar, par monsoon (July-August) dono jagah heavy rain la sakta hai — us window ko avoid karna better hai.

## Accessibility guests ke liye

Shimla, Chandigarh se closer hai aur road connectivity better hai — bade guest list ke saath ye practical factor important ho jaata hai, khaas kar older relatives ke liye.

Manali thoda door hai, aur road journey lamba ho sakta hai (Chandigarh se 7-8 ghante). Agar guest list chhoti hai aur experience-focused wedding chahiye, ye trade-off usually worth it hota hai.

## Photography ke nazariye se

Manali zyada dramatic, "epic" backdrops deta hai — snow peaks, dense forests, wide valleys. Shimla zyada intimate aur architectural frames deta hai — colonial buildings, narrow lanes, structured gardens.

Agar aapki wedding style bold aur landscape-heavy hai, Manali better match hai. Agar aap heritage aur elegant, timeless frames chahte hain, Shimla us direction mein better kaam karta hai.

## Kaunsa chunein?

Simple rule: agar guest list bada hai aur logistics priority hai, Shimla easier hoga. Agar aap chhota, intimate, landscape-driven celebration chahte hain aur travel time affordable hai, Manali zyada memorable ban sakta hai.
