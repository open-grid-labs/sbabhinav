"How much does a wedding photographer in Himachal cost?" is usually the first question we get, and it's also the hardest to answer with a single number — because the honest answer is "it depends," and it depends on a handful of specific things you can actually control. Here's what those things are, so you know what you're pricing when you ask for a quote.

## Number of coverage days

A single-day wedding is priced differently from a 3-4 day celebration with mehendi, haldi, sangeet, and the wedding itself. Most of the cost difference between quotes couples get isn't the photographer being more or less expensive — it's simply how many days and functions are actually being covered.

## Team size

One photographer covering a small, intimate wedding costs less than a two-photographer-plus-cinematographer team covering a large wedding across multiple venues at once. If you have overlapping events — the baraat and the bride's getting-ready happening simultaneously — you need more than one shooter to cover both without missing either.

## Travel and location

Shooting in your home city is simpler to price than a destination wedding in Manali or Shimla, which adds travel, accommodation, and sometimes extra days for scouting or weather buffers. If you're flying a team in from outside Himachal, factor this in separately from the core photography fee.

## Pre-wedding vs. wedding-day coverage

A pre-wedding shoot is usually quoted separately from wedding-day coverage, since it's a different kind of session — planned around locations and light rather than following a fixed event schedule. If you want both, ask whether bundling them brings the total down.

## Deliverables

This is where quotes can look similar on the surface but differ a lot underneath:

- How many edited photos are actually delivered, and in what timeframe
- Whether a highlight film or cinematic edit is included, or priced separately
- Whether you get high-resolution files or only web-sized ones
- Whether physical albums or prints are included or an add-on

## What to actually ask for in a quote

Rather than asking "what's your price," ask for a quote broken down by:

1. Coverage days and hours per day
2. Team size and roles (photography vs. cinematography)
3. Travel and stay, if applicable
4. Number of edited photos and delivery timeline
5. What's included vs. what's an add-on (albums, second shooter, drone, etc.)

This breakdown makes it possible to actually compare two photographers' quotes against each other, instead of comparing two numbers that mean different things.
